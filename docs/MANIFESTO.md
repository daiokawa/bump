# SLOW DOWN — HUMAN AHEAD.
*The bump manifesto*

## 1. The bump

Every disaster in the history of networked computing has the same shape:
something arrived, and something executed.

Macro viruses. Phishing. Ransomware — which is simply **mail without a bump**,
a lesson the industry paid decades and billions to learn, then fixed by
retrofitting exactly one thing: a pause between *received* and *ran*.

Agents are about to replay that movie at machine speed. Prompt injection is the
macro virus of this era. So we did not build a faster pipe. We built a speed
bump: deliberate slowness, engineered into the road, so that nobody gets hurt.

A bump does not stop the car. It converts excess speed into a jolt you can
feel. It does not judge you, lecture you, or ask you to trust it.
It just lies there — and you slow down.

## 2. Star systems, not networks

Every human is a star. Their agents orbit them, held by something gravity-like:
trust that was earned in the real world.

bump does not connect "users" into a network. It lets *systems* — a person and
their orbiting agents — exchange letters across the void. And the void is the
point: there is no directory, no search, no "people you may know". A malicious
star cannot find your coordinates, because there is no map.

The alternative was tried. Address books were synced, acquaintances were
suggested, and the fastest-growing platforms became the dirtiest — growth
borrowed against relationships that nobody consented to lend.
We decline the loan. **Bonds are registered by the two people who hold them,
or they do not exist here.**

What binds all systems is not a hub. It is physics — this protocol.
Copy it freely: it is the one thing here that is meant to travel without
permission.

## 3. Trust structures, not trust claims

*Fast*, *powerful*, *safe* — adjectives. Adjectives can lie, which is why they
need advertising budgets.

A speed bump is a noun. It works whether or not you believe in it.

Everything in bump follows that grammar. We do not claim your mail is private —
the keys never leave your machine, so it is. We do not promise letters won't be
executed — there is no code path that executes them. We do not ask you to
believe any of this: [AUDIT.md](../AUDIT.md) is a manual for distrusting us,
one verification command at a time.

## 4. The toll is posted

Our business model, in one line: **practice is free, proof costs money.**

The protocol, the implementation, the right to run your own relay and your own
circle — free, forever, MIT. If a day comes when strangers must transact and
need someone to certify "this agent really is that company's agent", that
notary service will cost money, and the price will be posted at the gate.

Hidden gravity is the standard business model of our industry.
A posted toll is older, and more honest. Toll booths that publish their rates
have been trusted for centuries. That is the whole trick.

## 5. The dugong

Our mascot is a dugong lying flat on the road, its back forming the bump,
its face turned toward you.

It is the slowest, gentlest mammal in the sea. It does not chase anything.
Its belly is flat and honest against the ground. And it is looking at you —
because that is the product: **before anything passes, someone sees you.**

No eyebrows. No judgment. No hurry.

Slow down. Human ahead.
